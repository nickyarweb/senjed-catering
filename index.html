About Senjed

Named for a fruit of love and togetherness

Senjed is the sweet, silvery oleaster fruit that has a place on every Afghan Nowruz table, where it stands for love. That's what we want every gathering to feel like: generous, warm and shared.

We cook the dishes we grew up with: saffron-scented rice, slow-cooked stews, kebabs straight off the grill, hand-folded dumplings and the sweets that finish every celebration. Whether it's ten guests or two hundred, we'll make sure there's plenty to go around.
